My research begins with a cognitive phenomenon — or its symptomatic breakdown in psychiatric illness — and asks what latent computations and representations underlie it. I use biophysical models to identify mesoscale signatures of these processes: brain oscillations that emerge from microscale circuit dynamics and can be measured across cortico-subcortical networks. These oscillations serve as the substrate I target for causal intervention using noninvasive methods such as transcranial alternating current stimulation and temporal interference stimulation. By manipulating specific rhythms in specific circuits, I probe the mechanistic link between biophysical processes and cognitive function — and develop principled therapeutic approaches for neuropsychiatric illness.

I am a Postdoctoral Associate working with [Rob Reinhart](http://reinhartlab.org) at Boston University. I completed my PhD in Psychology (concentration: Brain, Behavior & Cognition) at Boston University. Previously, I worked with [Devarajan Sridharan](https://cns.iisc.ac.in/sridhar/) at the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from Birla Institute of Technology & Science, Pilani, India.
